SIGMA Biology Universe — Episode 1: What Is Life?

Hook — Can it be alive?

Fire moves, changes and uses energy. A crystal grows, has structure and responds slightly. A robot moves, follows rules and can think using artificial intelligence. Why are they not considered alive?

Cinematic story

Billions of years ago, Earth was rock, water and energy. From simple chemicals, the first living things appeared. What made them different from everything else?

Aha moment

Life is not simply movement or growth. Life is organized complexity that can maintain itself and continue.

Core explanation

Life is characterized by organization, metabolism, homeostasis, growth, response, reproduction and adaptation.

Example comparison

A plant is living because it is organized, uses energy, maintains internal balance, grows, responds, reproduces and adapts over generations. A rock does not perform these coordinated processes.

IB skill focus

Observation and questioning: observe, make questions, investigate, test evidence and draw conclusions.

Real-world connection

Understanding life helps us develop medicines, protect biodiversity, improve agriculture and solve global challenges.
