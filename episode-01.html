SIGMA BIOLOGY · ARC 01 · EPISODE 01

WHAT IS LIFE?

The characteristics that distinguish living systems

01HOOK

Fire uses energy. Crystals grow. Robots move. Why are they not alive?

What evidence would convince you?

02DISCOVER

Life emerged from non-living chemistry, but living systems gained something remarkable: coordinated organization.

03AHA MOMENT

Life is an organized, self-maintaining system.

04CORE IDEA

Living systems coordinate:

  • Organization
  • Metabolism
  • Homeostasis
  • Growth and response
  • Reproduction and evolution

05COMPARE

LIVING

Cells work together, use energy, regulate internal conditions and carry genetic information.

NON-LIVING

May show one life-like trait, but not the coordinated system as a whole.

06IB SKILL

Observe → question → test evidence → draw a justified conclusion.

Avoid deciding from one trait alone.

07NEXT QUESTION

Viruses reproduce only inside host cells. Are they alive?

KEY TAKEAWAY

Life is defined by a coordinated system, not one isolated characteristic.

TODAY WE LEARNED

Organization + energy + regulation + information + evolution work together to make life possible.
